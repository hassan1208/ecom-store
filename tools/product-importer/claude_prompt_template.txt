=====================

one), replace the [PRODUCT INFO] section at the bottom with the real details
of the product you want written up, and send it. Claude will reply with one

since Claude doesn't know those.

Do this once per product, or paste several [PRODUCT INFO] sections in one
message to get several blocks back at once.
